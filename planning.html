Planning Analysis

Planning Analysis Sheet and Site Flowchart

This page explains the website goal, expected outcomes, page structure, required information, content sources, and overall navigation flow for the VIDIDIT website.

Website Goal

The goal of the VIDIDIT website is to present an educational technology solution that helps students move from simply receiving AI-generated answers to developing deeper understanding through explainable, adaptive, and story-based learning.

What Results Do We Want to See?

  • Clear explanation of the educational problem VIDIDIT addresses.
  • Better awareness of explainable AI as a learning support tool.
  • A structured website that presents the solution, community value, risks, and reflections.
  • Interest from students, educators, and institutions in responsible AI-supported learning.

Working Titles of Website Pages

  • Home
  • Problem
  • Solution
  • Community Engagement
  • Reflection
  • Market Risks
  • Planning Analysis
  • Contact

What Information Do We Need?

  • Information about the learning problem students face with AI tools.
  • Research on AI storytelling, multimodal learning, and explainable AI.
  • Examples of existing AI learning tools and related platforms.
  • Market risk and competitor information.
  • Community feedback, comments, or validation from real people.
  • Images, icons, and visual elements for website presentation.
