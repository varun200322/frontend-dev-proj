The website was handed over to a variety of people, and the comments helped us better understand our work.

Pragnyan (6th Grade Student)

- "I would definetly use a tool like this that simplifies my studies. Especially geometry when i need to analyse shapes, angles and edges. Creating a video, animating the explanations, is a great idea. Would definetly reccomend this to my friends."

Dr. Rashmi Sumanth (Former Principal Baldwins International)

This initiative has the potential to contribute positively toward more reflective, personalized, and learner-centered educational environments."
